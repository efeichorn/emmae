Emma Eichorn (b.1997) is an artist and educator, currently living in Brooklyn, NY. She grew up between the USA and the UK, attending the Rhode Island School of Design and graduating in 2021 with an honors degree in Painting. Eichorn received the Florence Leif travel award and has shown her works in group shows in both London and New York.
